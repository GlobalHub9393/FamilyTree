Current research focus

What happened to Louis Sr.?

His death year and obituary have not been proven. The working hypothesis is a Pennsylvania death around the 1920s, but this needs independent verification. There is a conflicting 1937 Social Security transcription, so a fixed death interval would be premature without checking that identity.

  • Confirm the 1920 and 1930 household records and whether Grace was described as widowed.
  • Search the Pennsylvania state death-certificate index for Louis/Ludwig Schreiner, especially Allegheny County.
  • Once a death date is known, search Pittsburgh newspaper death notices within several days of it.
  • Compare any certificate’s wife, occupation, birthplace and relatives before attaching it to our Louis.

Also unresolved: Louis Sr.’s parents and whether the April 1872 baptism in Lam records the same person. No proposed parents are shown in this family tree.
